Your information is used to:

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. LDF does not need to store your complete card, banking, or UPI credentials.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use services such as Google services, payment providers, website hosting, and WhatsApp to operate our tournaments.

We do not sell participant personal information. We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect.

6. Data Retention

We may retain tournament and registration information for as long as reasonably necessary for tournament administration, records, disputes, and legal requirements.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us through the Contact Us page.
