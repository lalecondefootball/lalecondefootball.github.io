LDF POLICIES

REFUND &
CANCELLATION

1. Player Cancellation

Players may request cancellation before the tournament begins. Refund eligibility depends on the circumstances and the applicable tournament rules.

2. Refund Eligibility

Approved refunds are generally limited to eligible cancellations made before the tournament starts. Once a tournament has started, entry fees are generally non-refundable.

3. Tournament Cancellation

If LDF cancels a tournament before it begins, eligible participants may receive a refund of their entry fee.

4. Minimum Player Requirement

If a tournament does not receive the required number of players, LDF may cancel or postpone the tournament. Eligible participants will be informed about the available refund or rescheduling options.

5. After the Tournament Starts

Entry fees are generally non-refundable after the tournament has started, including cases where a player fails to participate, leaves the tournament, or is disqualified for violating the rules.

6. Failed Payments

If a payment is debited but the registration is not successfully completed due to a payment or technical issue, the transaction may be reviewed with the payment provider before any refund is processed.

7. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method where possible. Processing time may depend on the payment provider and banking system.

8. Contact Us

For cancellation or refund requests, contact La Leçon de Football through our Contact Us page and provide your registration details.
