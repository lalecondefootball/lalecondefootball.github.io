CONDITIONS

1. Tournament Registration

Participants must provide accurate registration details, including their full name, WhatsApp number, and eFootball UID. In-Game Name is optional.

2. Entry Fee

Each tournament has its own entry fee, shown on the registration page. A slot is confirmed only after the payment and registration are verified.

3. Fair Play

LDF tournaments are skill-based eFootball competitions. Cheating, exploiting bugs, account sharing, result manipulation, or unfair play may result in disqualification.

4. Tournament Rules

Players must follow the tournament rules and instructions given by LDF administrators. Failure to join matches or follow instructions may result in a forfeit or disqualification.

5. Prizes

Prize amounts are displayed on each tournament page. LDF may verify participant details before distributing prizes.

6. Cancellation & Refunds

Tournament cancellations and refunds are handled according to our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

7. Changes

LDF may update tournament rules or these Terms when necessary. Updated versions will be published on this website.
